Anime Series

Naruto

Action Adventure Fantasy

Naruto Uzumaki is a spirited young ninja from the Hidden Leaf Village who grows up shunned because he carries the Nine-Tails fox spirit inside him. Despite the loneliness and rejection he faces, he never gives up on his dream of becoming Hokage—the strongest and most respected ninja in the village. Through relentless training, painful battles, and deep friendships, Naruto transforms from an outcast into one of the most powerful and inspiring ninjas of his generation.
