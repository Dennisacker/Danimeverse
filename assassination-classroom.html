Assassination Classroom

Comedy Action School

A powerful alien teacher threatens to destroy Earth unless his students can assassinate him first, but he becomes the best teacher they’ve ever had. As Class 3-E trains to kill him, they also learn life lessons, growth, and the true meaning of purpose. What begins as a mission to save the world slowly turns into something far more emotional and unexpected.
